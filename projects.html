Section 06/10 Circular City

Zero-Landfill Waste-to-Resource Cycle

How 4,300 metric tons of daily city refuse are converted into high-grade organic fertilizer and grid-fed green electricity.

1. Doorstep Segregation

92% segregation adherence at residential units into Wet, Dry, and Hazardous electronic categories.

2. IoT Fleet Logistics

340 electric compactors equipped with live GPS route optimization and load sensors.

3. Plasma Gasification

High-temperature thermal digestion turning combustible non-recyclables into synthesis gas.

4. 32MW Power Fed

Supplying non-stop green power back to city water pumping stations and electric bus depots.
