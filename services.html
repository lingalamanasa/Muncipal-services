Section 09/10 Help & Clarity

Frequently Asked Citizen Questions

Answers regarding online tax payments, complaint escalation steps, and certificate verifications.

How do I claim the 5% early-bird rebate on my municipal property tax?
All properties that clear their annual property tax liabilities in full before September 30 automatically receive the 5% rebate credited directly into the generated payment invoice.
What happens if my reported grievance is not fixed within the 48-hour SLA?
If an issue exceeds its designated SLA timeframe, Stackly automatically triggers an escalation alert to the Zonal Joint Commissioner and sends an automated apology SMS with the direct contact number of the supervisory inspector.
Can I verify the authenticity of a digital birth certificate offline?
Yes! Each PDF certificate generated through Stackly includes a cryptographically signed SHA-256 QR code. Anyone scanning this QR code will be redirected to confirm the original registry record.
