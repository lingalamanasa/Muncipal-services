Section 07/10 Statutory Freedom of Information

Right to Information (RTI Hub)

Exercise your statutory right to request administrative files, expenditure vouchers, tender logs, and departmental audit records.

Online RTI Filing in 3 Minutes

Any citizen can submit an RTI query online. The designated Public Information Officer (PIO) is mandated under law to respond with certified copies within 30 calendar days.

  • Statutory application fee: $1.50 (Waived for BPL card holders)
  • First Appellate Authority appointed for appeals within 15 days
  • Proactive Section 4(1)(b) disclosures updated monthly
