Section 02/10 Historical Legacy

A Century of Civic Evolution

Trace the milestones that transformed our historic settlement into an internationally celebrated smart metropolis.

1888

First Municipal Charter

Foundation of the Town Board to construct the first clean gravitational aqueduct and paved commerce quarter.

1964

Metropolitan Expansion

Consolidation of 5 peripheral boroughs into a single unified Municipal Corporation with 48 administrative wards.

2012

Pioneering Green Energy

Launch of mandatory rainwater harvesting bylaws and building the country's first 50MW municipal solar field.

2026

Stackly Digital OS

100% paperless e-governance with guaranteed 48-hour SLA resolution and AI municipal dispatch.

Section 03/10 Core Principles

Our Guiding Civic Pillars

Every resolution passed and public dollar spent adheres strictly to four fundamental institutional values.

Absolute Transparency

Real-time public tracking of municipal budgets, contract awards, audit accounts, and SLA performance.

Environmental Stewardship

Aggressive decarbonization, zero-waste to landfill programs, and safeguarding 18 urban wetland ecosystems.

Citizen Inclusivity

Universal accessibility for all abilities, multilingual assistance, and participatory community budgeting.

Continuous Innovation

Leveraging AI dispatch, IoT sensors, and open data to predict urban bottlenecks and optimize routes.

Section 07/10 e-Governance Milestones

The Stackly Digital Transformation

How our migration to the Stackly civic operating system eliminated paper bureaucracy and accelerated citizen service turnarounds.
